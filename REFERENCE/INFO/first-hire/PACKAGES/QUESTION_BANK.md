# Founder question bank

Questions startups ask as they turn into businesses, grouped by topic. ✅ = an existing package answers it. ★ = a proposed next package.

## HR / people
- ✅ Full-time hire or contractor? (EMPLOYEE_VS_CONTRACTOR)
- ✅ What paperwork do I need on day 1? (FIRST_HIRE_CHECKLIST)
- ✅ Exempt vs. non-exempt: do I have to pay my developer overtime? What's the salary threshold? (FLSA; GA has no state overtime law, so verify) (EXEMPT_VS_NONEXEMPT_PAY_RULES)
- ✅ Can I pay in equity instead of salary, or pay below minimum wage while we're pre-revenue? (EXEMPT_VS_NONEXEMPT_PAY_RULES)
- ✅ Can I hire interns, or have people work unpaid? (EXEMPT_VS_NONEXEMPT_PAY_RULES)
- ★ What goes in an offer letter? Do I need an employee handbook?
- ★ Can I use non-competes in GA? (GA Restrictive Covenants Act, O.C.G.A. 13-8-50 et seq.)
- ✅ How do I hire someone remote in another state? (registering in that state, its withholding and unemployment tax) (REMOTE_AND_OUT_OF_STATE_HIRES)
- ✅ How do I hire someone outside the US? (EOR, contractor-of-record) (REMOTE_AND_OUT_OF_STATE_HIRES)
- ✅ Can I sponsor a visa (H-1B, O-1) or hire an F-1 student on OPT/CPT? (REMOTE_AND_OUT_OF_STATE_HIRES)
- ✅ Firing: what do I owe? Final paycheck timing, COBRA / GA continuation, unemployment claims, separation notice (GA DOL-800) (FIRING_AND_OFFBOARDING_GA)
- ✅ When do federal laws start applying by headcount? (Title VII 15, ADEA 20, COBRA 20, ADA 15, FMLA 50, ACA 50, WARN 100, EEO-1 100) (HEADCOUNT_TRIGGERS_AND_COMPLIANCE_CALENDAR)
- Paid leave: does GA require PTO or sick leave? What about GA's kin care rule and jury/voting leave?
- Background checks and drug testing (FCRA; GA Drug-Free Workplace program discount)

## Benefits
- ✅ Do I have to offer health insurance? What are QSEHRA / ICHRA and small-group plans? (PAYROLL_PEO_AND_BENEFITS)
- ✅ Retirement: SIMPLE IRA vs. Safe Harbor 401(k). Does GA have a state auto-IRA mandate? (verify; GA doesn't appear to) (PAYROLL_PEO_AND_BENEFITS)
- Can founders get benefits through the company? Do LLC owners get W-2 benefits?

## Taxes
- ✅ What payroll taxes do I withhold and pay in GA? (GA_EMPLOYER_TAXES)
- ✅ How do founders pay themselves? Owner's draw vs. salary, S-corp election and "reasonable compensation", guaranteed payments (PAYING_FOUNDERS)
- ★ LLC vs. S-corp vs. C-corp taxation, and when to switch
- Estimated quarterly taxes for founders (1040-ES, GA 500-ES)
- GA sales and use tax: is SaaS or digital product taxable in GA? (verify; this varies)
- GA net worth tax and GA income tax filings (600, 700 for partnerships)
- Tax credits: R&D credit, including the payroll tax offset for startups (Form 6765); GA job tax credits, GA R&D credit, angel investor credit (verify whether it's still active)
- 1099s: who gets one and when
- Business deductions: home office, equipment, Section 179

## GA regulations and licensing
- ✅ DE entity operating in GA (DELAWARE_ENTITY_IN_GEORGIA)
- ✅ Do I need a city or county business license or occupation tax certificate? (Atlanta, Fulton, DeKalb, etc.) (LOCAL_BUSINESS_LICENSES_METRO_ATLANTA)
- Annual registration with SOS, registered agent, keeping good standing
- Professional licensing, if the business is in a regulated field
- Zoning for a home-based business

## Compliance
- ✅ Annual compliance calendar: federal, GA, DE, and local deadlines in one place (HEADCOUNT_TRIGGERS_AND_COMPLIANCE_CALENDAR)
- Required posters, including remote-worker delivery
- I-9 retention and audits; E-Verify at 11+ employees
- Workers' comp: when it's required, how to buy it, and penalties
- BOI reporting (FinCEN CTA): does it still apply to US companies? (verify; the 2025 rule exempted domestic companies)
- Data privacy and security: GA breach notification law (O.C.G.A. 10-1-912), handling SSNs, collecting customer data
- Accessibility (ADA) for websites
- Recordkeeping: how long to keep payroll and personnel records

## Practices and operations
- ✅ Payroll providers and PEOs: when to use which, and what they handle (PAYROLL_PEO_AND_BENEFITS)
- Business insurance: GL, E&O / professional, cyber, D&O, workers' comp (the local guide is the 2019 edition, so refresh it)
- Bookkeeping and choosing a CPA
- Banking: separating business and personal funds to protect the liability shield
- Founder agreements, vesting for founders, and 83(b) (partly covered in EQUITY_FOR_EARLY_HIRES)
- IP: trademarks, protecting code, open-source license obligations
- Contracts: MSA, SOW, NDA templates
- Fundraising basics: SAFEs, Reg D / Form D, GA notice filing (verify)
