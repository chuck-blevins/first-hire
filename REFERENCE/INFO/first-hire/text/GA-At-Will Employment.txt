At-Will Employment
All SAO employees, full-time or part-time, are hired as at-will employees. At-will employment
means that the SAO can separate an employee from employment at any time, for no reason or for
any reason, except an illegal reason. It also means an employee is free to resign at any time for no
reason or for any reason.
When an employee makes a decision to resign from at-will employment, it is preferable, but not
required, that the employee give sufficient notice; a two-week notice is the generally accepted
professional standard.
