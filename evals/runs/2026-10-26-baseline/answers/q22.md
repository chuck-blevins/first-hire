General information, not legal or tax advice.

With no employees, you have no payroll filings. Your deadlines between now and April come from the contractors you paid, your two states, and your corporate tax return. In date order:

| Date | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Now** | Collect a **Form W-9** from each contractor you haven't already | You need their TIN to file the 1099. [IRS W-9](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-9) |
| **Dec 15, 2026** | Q4 corporate estimated tax (federal and GA) | Only if you expect to owe income tax. Pre-revenue companies usually don't. [GA DOR corporate due dates](https://dor.georgia.gov/corporate-due-dates) |
| **Feb 1, 2027** | **Form 1099-NEC** to each contractor and to the IRS | The rule is Jan 31, but Jan 31, 2027 is a Sunday. **For payments made in 2026, the threshold is $2,000 or more per contractor, not the old $600.** If you file 10 or more information returns of any type, you must e-file. [1099-NEC instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099mec) |
| Feb 1, 2027 | GA Form G-1003 | **Only if you withheld Georgia tax** from a contractor. Most founders didn't, so most can skip it. [GA DOR 2026 Employer's Tax Guide](https://dor.georgia.gov/document/document/2026-employers-tax-guide-updated-june-2026/download) |
| **Mid-Feb / Apr 1, 2027** | **City of Atlanta** occupation tax certificate renewal: file by Feb 15, pay by Apr 1 | Applies only if you're inside Atlanta city limits. Feb 15, 2027 is Presidents' Day. The late-filing fee is reported as $500. **Unverified:** the city's site blocks automated checks, so confirm with the [Office of Revenue](https://www.atlantaga.gov/government/departments/finance/office-of-revenue). |
| **Mar 1, 2027** | **Delaware franchise tax + annual report** | The annual report fee is $50. The penalty is $200 plus 1.5% a month. **Before you pay, recalculate with the Assumed Par Value Capital method.** The default bill on 10M authorized shares can be about $85K, but it often recalculates to the **$400 minimum**. [DE: Pay taxes](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/); [calculator](https://corp.delaware.gov/frtaxcalc/) |
| **Apr 1, 2027** | **GA annual registration** ($50, window opens Jan 1) | This applies if your Delaware corp is registered in Georgia as a foreign corporation. If you have an Atlanta office and **haven't** registered, do that first: $225, and a $500 penalty applies after 30 days. **Unverified:** sos.ga.gov blocks automated checks, so confirm at the [GA SOS annual registration page](https://sos.ga.gov/how-to-guide/how-file-annual-registration). |
| **Apr 15, 2027** | **Form 1120** (federal) and **GA Form 600**, which includes the GA net worth tax. You can file extensions instead | You must file even with no revenue. Net worth of $100K or less owes no net worth tax but still files. [GA DOR](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/business-taxes/corporate-income-and-net-worth-tax) |

Things that are **not** due yet: Form 941, Form 940, GA G-7, GA unemployment filings, and W-2s. They start with your first W-2 employee. Note that you and any co-founder count as employees too if you take a salary.

**If your "contractors" worked set hours on your core product**, ask a CPA whether they were really employees before you file the 1099s. Also make sure each one signed an **IP assignment**. Investors will check for it.

**Sources:** I re-checked the 1099-NEC $2,000 threshold, the Jan 31 rule, and the e-file threshold live on IRS.gov today. I also re-checked the Delaware March 1 due date and fees, the GA corporate due dates, and the G-1003 rule. The Atlanta license and GA annual registration figures come from our 2026-10-25 package and couldn't be fetched live.

A CPA can file the 1120 and GA 600 and recalculate the Delaware franchise tax. That's usually money well spent.
