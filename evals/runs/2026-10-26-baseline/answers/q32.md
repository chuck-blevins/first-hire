General information, not legal or tax advice.

**First, confirm whether the office address is inside the City of Peachtree Corners.** Peachtree Corners is an incorporated city with its own business license, an occupation tax certificate or OTC ([city page](https://peachtreecornersga.gov/317/Business-License)). Gwinnett County licenses only businesses in *unincorporated* Gwinnett and sends city addresses to the city ([Gwinnett](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/planning-development/services/business-license)). Run the exact suite address through Gwinnett's address lookup on that page, because a "Peachtree Corners" mailing address isn't proof either way.

**Before renewals, you need a license for the new location.** A new office generally needs its own certificate. If you were licensed somewhere else, such as a founder's home, close that certificate and apply where the office is.

**If the office is in the City of Peachtree Corners** (checked 2026-10-26):
- **New-location fees:** Zoning Use Approval is **$75** and a city inspection is **$100**, both non-refundable. Commercial space may also need a Gwinnett Fire Certificate of Occupancy. Ask the city when you apply.
- **Renewal:** it's annual and done online through BS&A, or on a paper application you request by email. **The city's page doesn't publish a renewal deadline or late penalties,** and I couldn't find them on an official source. Ask the Finance Department at 678-691-1200 or businesslicense@peachtreecornersga.gov and put the date on your calendar. Don't assume another city's dates apply.

**If the office is in unincorporated Gwinnett** (confirmed on Gwinnett's page 2026-10-26):
- Renew by **February 15**.
- After Feb 15: a **$25 late fee**.
- Paid after **March 31**: an added **10%** of the total due.
- Interest of **1.5% per month**.

**For both:**
- **E-Verify affidavit:** every application and renewal includes one. You have 7 employees, so you sign the exemption affidavit for employers with 10 or fewer full-time employees counted on January 1 ([O.C.G.A. § 36-60-6](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-36-local-government/ga-code-sect-36-60-6/)). If you're at **11 or more full-time employees on Jan 1**, enroll at [e-verify.gov](https://www.e-verify.gov/) *before* you renew. Plan for that if you're hiring soon.
- **What you'll report:** renewals usually ask for prior-year gross receipts and employee count, and the tax grows with both.
- **Timing:** renewal season (Jan–Mar) overlaps with W-2s (due Feb 1, 2027) and your Q4 941. Set reminders 2 weeks before each deadline.
- **Lateness:** beyond penalties and interest, operating without a current certificate can lead to a citation.

If you end up with offices in more than one jurisdiction, or need to apportion gross receipts, get a CPA involved.
