**That's outside what I can help with.** I cover employer obligations for startup founders: hiring, payroll taxes, and Georgia and federal employment rules. Your filing status and whether you can claim your daughter's daycare are questions about your personal income tax. The right answer depends on both spouses' incomes, other deductions, any dependent-care FSA, and state rules, so I won't guess.

**Where to get a good answer:**
- **A CPA or enrolled agent:** the best option, especially as a founder, since your startup income or losses can change the math. They can run both filing scenarios side by side.
- **IRS Interactive Tax Assistant:** free official tools that walk you through filing status and dependent-care questions, at [irs.gov/help/ita](https://www.irs.gov/help/ita).
- **IRS Free File or VITA:** free filing help if you qualify, at [irs.gov/filing/free-file-do-your-federal-taxes-for-free](https://www.irs.gov/filing/free-file-do-your-federal-taxes-for-free).
