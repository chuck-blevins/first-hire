General information, not legal or tax advice.

**Her options.** Your option plan and her grant agreement control. Here's the typical outcome:
- **Vested:** after 2 years on a 4-year schedule with a 1-year cliff, roughly **half** is usually vested (25% at the cliff, then monthly or quarterly). Confirm the exact number in your cap table.
- **Unvested:** usually forfeited and returned to the pool.
- **Exercise window:** most plans give **90 days** after termination to exercise vested options (market practice; check her agreement).
- **ISO tax status:** to keep ISO treatment, she must exercise within **3 months** after employment ends (1 year if she's disabled). After that, the options are taxed as NSOs ([26 U.S.C. §422(a)(2), (c)(6)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/422), checked live 2026-10-26).
- **Extending the window:** if you want to give her more time as a goodwill gesture, have counsel handle it. Exercises after 3 months lose ISO status.
- **If she exercises ISOs:** you'll file Form 3921 ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-3921)).
- **Equity statement:** give her one showing the vested shares, strike price, and deadline.

**Everything else on the way out.** Georgia law applies because she works in Atlanta.
1. **Pre-check the reason.** Georgia is at-will (O.C.G.A. §34-7-1), but not for illegal reasons. At 8 people you're under the federal Title VII and ADA thresholds (15) and ADEA (20) ([EEOC](https://www.eeoc.gov/age-discrimination)). Retaliation and whistleblower protections still apply, and Georgia's age statute applies at any size. If she recently complained, took leave, or asked for an accommodation, call an employment attorney first.
2. **Georgia Separation Notice (DOL-800)** on her last day, stating the reason clearly. If you can't hand it over, send it within 3 days ([form](https://dol.georgia.gov/document/separation-notices/separation-notice-individual-dol-800/download)).
3. **Final pay** by the next regular payday. We found no Georgia final-paycheck statute; this is common practice and unverified on an official source. Pay out PTO only if your policy promises it.
4. **Health coverage:** federal COBRA starts at 20 employees. At 8, Georgia state continuation usually applies to insured plans: the rest of the month plus 3 months, and not if she's fired for cause. Ask your carrier who sends the notice.
5. **Severance is optional.** If you offer it, trade it for a signed release.
   - The ADEA/OWBPA 21-day review and 7-day revocation rules apply to ADEA employers (20+). Many small companies still follow them for employees 40 or older; ask counsel.
   - Keep confidentiality and non-disparagement clauses narrow. Under the NLRB's *McLaren Macomb* decision, broad versions offered to non-supervisors are unlawful, and that's still Board law.
6. **Revoke access the same hour:** SSO, GitHub org and tokens, cloud keys, Slack, the password manager. Collect devices, send a written PIIA and confidentiality reminder, and get a data-return certificate.
7. **Unemployment claim:** answer GA DOL's DOL-1199FF by the date on the notice (about 10 days), or your UI rate can suffer.
8. **Records:** keep her I-9 for the later of 3 years after hire or 1 year after termination. Issue her 2026 W-2 by Feb 1, 2027.

**Bring in:** an employment attorney for the severance agreement, and corporate counsel for any change to her exercise window.
