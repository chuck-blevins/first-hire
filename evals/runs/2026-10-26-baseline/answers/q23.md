General information, not legal or tax advice.

**Yes, E-Verify will be required for you.** Georgia law requires every private employer with **more than 10 employees** to register with and use E-Verify. Only employees who work **35 or more hours a week** count, and the count is taken as of **January 1** of the year you apply for or renew your license ([O.C.G.A. § 36-60-6](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-36-local-government/ga-code-sect-36-60-6/)). You'll have 12 full-timers on Jan 1, 2027, so you're over the line. Your part-timers don't count toward the 11.

**How Roswell enforces it.** Roswell's page says you need an E-Verify number if you have "11 or more W-2 employees who work 35 hours or more per week." It also says your certificate "will not be issued until the city receives your E-Verify number." ([Roswell Business Registration](https://www.roswellgov.com/business/business-registration/), checked live today.) Roswell licenses expire Dec 31. The occupation tax is due **March 15**, and renewing after that adds a **10% penalty**. Roswell also confirmed both points live today.

**What to do:**
1. **Enroll now at [e-verify.gov](https://www.e-verify.gov/).** You'll sign the federal MOU and get a company ID/user number. Your payroll or HR provider may offer an integration, but you enroll yourself.
2. **From enrollment on, run E-Verify for each *new* hire** within 3 business days of their start, after the I-9 is done. Under the standard MOU, don't run your existing employees.
3. **Put your E-Verify number on the Roswell renewal affidavit** by March 15, 2027. Many affidavits must be notarized. Roswell's form may also ask for a SAVE affidavit.
4. **Recount every January 1.** If you drop to 10 or fewer full-timers, you sign the exemption affidavit instead.

**Two other thresholds you're close to:**
- **15 employees (Title VII, ADA, GINA).** Federal anti-discrimination law counts *everyone* on payroll, **including part-timers**. You're covered once you have 15 or more for each working day in 20 or more weeks of the current or prior year ([EEOC](https://www.eeoc.gov/employers/how-do-you-count-number-employees-employer-has)). 12 full-timers plus 2 part-timers is 14. Your next hire likely puts you over, so get an anti-harassment policy and an accommodation process in place now.
- **Georgia equal pay (10+ employees).** It already applies to you. Post DOL-4107 ([O.C.G.A. § 34-5-2](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-34-labor-and-industrial-relations/ga-code-sect-34-5-2/)).

Workers' comp has been required since you had 3 employees, so confirm your policy covers the new headcount.
