*General information, not legal or tax advice.*

Congrats. With a Dec 1 start you have about five weeks, which is enough time if you start now. Here's the order. Steps 1–5 take a few hours in total.

**Now (before the offer goes out)**
1. **Confirm this is a W-2 employee, not a 1099 contractor.** A full-time dev building your core product on your schedule is an employee.
2. **EIN.** If the corp doesn't have one yet, get it free from the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number). Never pay a site for an EIN.
3. **EFTPS.** Enroll at [eftps.gov](https://www.eftps.gov) now. The PIN comes by mail and takes about a week.
4. **GA withholding number.** Register in the [Georgia Tax Center](https://gtc.dor.ga.gov/_/). You'll need a NAICS code. The number usually arrives by email in about 15 minutes ([GA DOR](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/register-new-business-georgia)).
5. **Payroll provider.** Pick one at least a week before your first payday. Ask it in writing which deposits and filings it submits for you. You stay legally responsible either way.
6. **Offer letter and PIIA.** The offer letter should be at-will and state pay and exempt status. The PIIA (IP assignment) must be signed by day 1 so the code belongs to the company. Have an attorney review both templates.

**Day 1 to day 10**
7. **I-9.** The employee completes Section 1 by day 1. You complete Section 2 within 3 business days. The current edition is 01/20/25 ([USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/i-9)). Keep it on file; you don't send it anywhere.
8. **W-4 and G-4.** Collect both ([W-4](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-4), [G-4](https://dor.georgia.gov/g-4-employee-withholding)).
9. **New-hire report.** File it within 10 days at [ga-newhire.com](https://www.ga-newhire.com/) ([FAQ](https://newhire-reporting.com/GA-Newhire/FAQ.aspx)). Many payroll providers do this for you.
10. **Posters.** Put up the GA DOL and federal posters ([GDOL list](https://dol.georgia.gov/laws-and-rules/gdol-required-workplace-posters)).

**Right after the first payroll**
11. **GA DOL unemployment account.** GA DOL says to file the DOL-1A "immediately following the payment of the first Georgia payroll" ([GA DOL FAQ](https://dol.georgia.gov/faqs-employers/employers-faqs-unemployment-insurance)). Use "Online Employer Tax Registration" on [GA DOL Online Services](https://dol.georgia.gov/online-services). A new employer pays 2.70% on the first $9,500 of each employee's wages.

**Ongoing**
- **Federal deposits.** In your first year you deposit monthly through EFTPS, by the 15th of the following month. December's deposit is due Jan 15.
- **Year-end filings.** Q4 Form 941, GA G-7, and GA DOL-4N are all due Feb 1, 2027. W-2s plus GA G-1003 are also due Feb 1, 2027, because Jan 31 falls on a Sunday.

**Things that can wait**
- **Workers' comp.** It's required at 3 regular employees, and corporate officers count, so you plus one hire makes 2 ([SBWC FAQ](https://sbwc.georgia.gov/frequently-asked-questions/workers-compensation-insurance-faqs)). It isn't mandatory yet, but it's cheap and worth buying anyway. Hire #2 makes it mandatory.
- **E-Verify.** It's optional at 10 or fewer employees. Your City of Atlanta business license renewal may ask for the exemption affidavit.

**Bring in a professional:** a CPA if you'll also put yourself on payroll, and an employment attorney for the offer letter and PIIA. If you'll grant equity, the board has to approve it first.
