General information, not legal or tax advice.

**How bad? Fixable, but register now.** A foreign LLC with a Georgia office and Georgia employees should hold a Georgia certificate of authority. Under O.C.G.A. §14-11-711 ([FindLaw](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-14-corporations-partnerships-and-associations/ga-code-sect-14-11-711/), checked live 2026-10-26):
- **Penalty:** you owe the fees you would have paid, plus a **$500 penalty**, because you're past 30 days. Georgia's Secretary of State says statutory penalties can't be waived.
- **Lawsuits:** you **can't bring a lawsuit in a Georgia court** until you register. You can still defend yourself if sued.
- **Contracts:** your contracts and past acts **stay valid**.

**To register:**
1. Order a **Delaware certificate of good standing** at [corp.delaware.gov](https://corp.delaware.gov/). Georgia expects one less than 90 days old. It's unconfirmed whether LLCs strictly need it, so get one anyway.
2. Appoint a **Georgia registered agent** with a Georgia street address.
3. File the application for a certificate of authority with the [GA Secretary of State](https://sos.ga.gov/index.php/corporations): **$225** by statute. I couldn't verify the fee live because sos.ga.gov blocks automated access; it's from our package, last checked 2026-10-25, so confirm at the link.
4. After that, calendar Georgia annual registration (**$50**, Jan 1 – Apr 1).

**More important: check your payroll accounts.** With 3 W-2 employees in Atlanta, confirm two things. If either is missing, it's a bigger problem than the SOS penalty, and you should call a CPA right away.
- Your payroll provider has been withholding Georgia income tax under a **GA DOR withholding account** ([Georgia Tax Center](https://gtc.dor.ga.gov/_/)).
- You have a **GA DOL unemployment account** ([GA DOL Employer Portal](https://eresponse.gdol.ga.gov/idp/sso/employer/login)).

Also confirm two more items:
- **Workers' comp:** required at 3 or more employees, and LLC members count toward the 3 ([SBWC](https://sbwc.georgia.gov/employer-information), checked live).
- **City of Atlanta business license** for the Midtown office.

**Delaware LLC annual tax: your $300 note is out of date going forward.**
- **$300** was the tax for the **2025** tax year, due June 1, 2026.
- Delaware's HB 400 raised it to **$400** starting with the **2026** tax year. That first $400 payment is due **June 1, 2027**.
- Delaware's tax-instructions page now lists **$400**, due June 1, with a **$200 late penalty plus 1.5% interest per month** ([DE LLC tax instructions](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/), checked live 2026-10-26).
- The live page doesn't state the effective tax year. The 2026 start date comes from secondary reporting ([Withum](https://www.withum.com/resources/delaware-businesses-face-increased-annual-taxes-and-filing-costs/)).
- No Delaware LLC annual report is required.

**Bring in:** a corporate attorney or registration service to file the Georgia registration quickly. If any payroll registrations are missing, bring in a CPA too. If you plan to raise VC, also ask about converting to a C-corp.
