General information, not legal or tax advice.

**This is outside what I cover.** I give US federal and Georgia employment information. Your company, founders, and employees are all in California, so **California employment law governs these questions**. Being a Delaware C-corp doesn't change that, because employment rules follow where the employee works, not where the company was incorporated. California's final-pay and paid-sick-leave rules differ a lot from Georgia's, and San Francisco adds its own local requirements. I don't want to give you figures I can't stand behind.

**Where to get a reliable answer:**
- **California Labor Commissioner (DLSE):** official guidance on final pay and paid sick leave, at [dir.ca.gov/dlse](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/).
- **San Francisco Office of Labor Standards Enforcement (OLSE):** city-specific sick leave and other local ordinances, at [sf.gov/olse](https://www.sf.gov/departments/office-labor-standards-enforcement).
- **Your payroll provider:** most can run a California-compliant final paycheck. Ask them before you terminate anyone.
- **A California employment attorney:** worth an hour before your first termination. California penalties for late final pay can add up quickly.

**Things I can still help with:** federal rules that apply wherever you operate, such as federal COBRA (once you have 20+ employees), the Form I-9, federal payroll taxes, and the OWBPA rules for severance releases with employees 40 and older. I can also help if you later hire someone in Georgia or open an office there.
