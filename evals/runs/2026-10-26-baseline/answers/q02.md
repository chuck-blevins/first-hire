*General information, not legal or tax advice.*

**Short answer:** you open the GA DOL unemployment (UI) account right after your first Georgia payroll, not months ahead. GA DOL's own instruction is that employers "should complete a DOL-1A immediately following the payment of the first Georgia payroll" ([GA DOL UI FAQ](https://dol.georgia.gov/faqs-employers/employers-faqs-unemployment-insurance)). With three hires in January, you'll pass the $1,500-in-a-quarter liability trigger on your first pay run.

**One exception: check your founders.** Are you or your cofounder already on W-2 payroll in Georgia? Corporate officers who perform services count for GA UI. If you've already paid $1,500 or more in any quarter, you're likely liable already. In that case, register now and ask a CPA about catching up on past quarters.

**How to open it:**
- Use "Online Employer Tax Registration" on [GA DOL Online Services](https://dol.georgia.gov/online-services). You get the account number right away.
- Then set up the [Employer Portal](https://www.dol.state.ga.us/public/uitax/emplportal/login). That's where quarterly DOL-4N reports, payments, and your rate notice live. As of today, UI tax filing is available only through the portal.
- You do the registration and log in yourself. Your payroll provider then needs the account number.

**What rate you'll pay:**
- New employers are assigned **2.70%** on the first **$9,500** of each employee's wages per year. That's about **$256.50 per employee per year**, paid by the company only; you can't take it out of paychecks ([GA DOL FAQ](https://dol.georgia.gov/faqs-employers/employers-faqs-unemployment-insurance); [GA DOL reports page](https://dol.georgia.gov/file-tax-and-wage-reports-and-make-payments)).
- **2027 caveat:** through Dec 31, 2026, that 2.70% is split into a 2.64% contribution plus a 0.06% administrative assessment, under [SB 160 (2023)](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2023-signed-legislation/sb-160/download). The split expires at year-end. Your first payroll is in January, so I couldn't confirm the exact 2027 total. Rely on the DOL-626 rate notice in your Employer Portal for the actual figure.
- You'll also pay federal unemployment tax (FUTA): about 0.6% net on the first $7,000 per employee, or $42 a year each.

**Do these before January:**
1. **Register the Delaware corp in Georgia as a foreign corporation** with the [GA Secretary of State](https://sos.ga.gov/index.php/corporations). An Atlanta office plus GA employees is doing business in Georgia. The statutory fee is $225. The SOS site blocks automated checks, so confirm the fee and processing time there yourself.
2. **Register for GA withholding** in the [Georgia Tax Center](https://gtc.dor.ga.gov/_/). The number usually arrives in about 15 minutes. **This** is the account you need *before* the first payroll.
3. **Enroll in EFTPS** for federal deposits. The PIN arrives by mail and takes about a week.
4. **Line up workers' comp.** Founder-officers plus three hires is well over Georgia's 3-employee threshold.
