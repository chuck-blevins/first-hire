*General information, not legal or tax advice.*

**On the logins:** please don't send them. Never share Georgia Tax Center or EFTPS credentials with anyone, me included. I can't log in or file for you. What I can do is give you the list so you can file everything yourself in about an hour. Or a payroll provider can take this over from Q4 on.

**You heard right: Oct 31, 2026 is a Saturday.** When a due date lands on a weekend, it moves to the next business day, so most Q3 deadlines become **Monday, Nov 2, 2026**.

| Filing | Where | Due |
|---|---|---|
| **Form 941** (Q3 federal payroll return) | IRS (e-file or mail); any balance through [EFTPS](https://www.eftps.gov) | **Nov 2**. If you made every Q3 deposit in full and on time, the IRS gives you until **Nov 10** ([IRS due dates](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employment-tax-due-dates)). |
| **FUTA deposit** | EFTPS | **Nov 2**, but only if your FUTA owed so far this year, including amounts carried over from earlier quarters, is more than $500. With 4 people, most of the $7,000-per-person wage base was likely used up in Q1–Q2, so you may owe little or nothing. |
| **GA Form G-7** (Q3 withholding return and payment) | [Georgia Tax Center](https://gtc.dor.ga.gov/_/) | **Nov 2, 2026**. The 2026 GA Employer's Tax Guide lists "3RD QTR 2026 G-7 RETURN 11/02/2026." If DOR set you up as a monthly payer, your monthly payments were due the 15th, and the G-7 is the return that reconciles them. |
| **GA DOL-4N** (unemployment tax and wage report, plus payment) | [GA DOL Employer Portal](https://www.dol.state.ga.us/public/uitax/emplportal/login) | **Nov 2**. GA DOL says reports due on a weekend are "due by the next business day" ([GA DOL](https://dol.georgia.gov/file-tax-and-wage-reports-and-make-payments)). |

**Also check:** your September federal deposit (as a monthly depositor) was due **Oct 15**. If you missed it, deposit it now. The penalty grows the longer it's late.

**Coming up next:**
- **Nov 15** (rolls to Nov 16, since the 15th is a Sunday): the federal deposit for October paydays.
- **Feb 1, 2027:** Q4 Form 941, G-7 and DOL-4N; W-2s to employees and the SSA; GA G-1003; and Form 940. Jan 31, 2027 is a Sunday, so all of these move to Feb 1.

**My honest take:** with 4 employees, a payroll provider that files all of this for you usually costs less than one late penalty. If you switch, ask the provider in writing which filings it will submit. You remain legally responsible for any it misses.
