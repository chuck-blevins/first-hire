**Yes, you very likely need one, even with zero revenue and a home office.** In Georgia, a "business license" is usually an **occupation tax certificate (OTC)**. You get it from the city if your address is inside city limits, or from the county if it's unincorporated ([O.C.G.A. § 48-13-6](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-48-revenue-and-taxation/ga-code-sect-48-13-6/)). Most jurisdictions charge a minimum tax and an administrative fee even at $0 in revenue.

**Step 1: Confirm which jurisdiction you're in.** A "Decatur" mailing address doesn't mean you're in the City of Decatur. Many of those addresses are in unincorporated DeKalb. Check your street address against the DeKalb County or City of Decatur boundary, or call DeKalb's license office at 404-371-2155, option 5.

**If you're in unincorporated DeKalb** ([DeKalb Business License](https://www.dekalbcountyga.gov/planning-and-sustainability/business-and-alcohol-license-0)):
- **Cost:** a **$75 administrative fee** plus a **$50 minimum tax**. Tax on gross receipts applies only above $20,000, and there is a per-worker fee that counts you as the owner. These figures are from DeKalb's [2026 renewal notice](https://dekalbcountyga.gov/sites/default/files/2026-01/2026%20RENEWAL%20APPLICATION.pdf), as last verified on 2026-10-25. I couldn't re-read the PDF today, so confirm the amounts, including the per-worker fee, with the office.
- **Home-based steps** (confirmed on DeKalb's page today): file the **Home-Based Business Application** and comply with zoning. Every application goes through a zoning review of about 7–10 business days. New applications are online only, at [epermits.dekalbcountyga.gov](https://epermits.dekalbcountyga.gov).
- **Affidavits:** sign the E-Verify exemption affidavit (you have 10 or fewer employees) and the SAVE affidavit. They're usually notarized.
- **Renewal:** under the 2026 schedule, you file by **Feb 1** and pay by **Apr 15**. Late-filing penalties start at $100. Expect 2027 to follow the same pattern, but confirm when the notice comes out.

**If you're inside the City of Decatur** ([Decatur Business License](https://decaturga.com/adminservices/page/business-license)): the city requires an OTC plus an "Application for Home-Based Business Occupancy." Secondary sources say fees run **$170–$460 depending on class, plus a $25 admin fee**, with half the tax if you start after July 1, and renewal due by **Jan 31**. These figures are **unverified**, because the city's site blocks automated checks. Call the city to confirm.

**Before either application:** if your company is a Delaware or other out-of-state entity, register it with the Georgia Secretary of State first. You'll also need your EIN, your NAICS code, and a government ID.

If you later hire someone who would work at your house, check the home-occupation rules first. They often bar non-resident employees on site.
